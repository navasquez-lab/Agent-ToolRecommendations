# HubSpot AI Agent Recommender — Contracts New Hire Training

An interactive quiz that helps new HubSpot Contracts team members identify the right AI agents and tools to recommend based on a customer's Hubs, tier, AI maturity, and pain points.

## What it does
Answer 4 questions about your customer and get:
- **Top 2 AI agent recommendations** ranked by fit, availability, and reliability
- **Relevant tools** matched to pain points, with availability and pricing
- **Discovery questions** to ask before recommending customer-facing agents
- **Upsell signals** when a customer is close to unlocking a more powerful option
